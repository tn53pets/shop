Spaces Built for Tails, Paws, and Peace of Mind

At TN-53 Pets, we didn’t just build a boarding center; we crafted a home-away-from-home from the ground up. We know that a strange environment can be stressful, so we’ve engineered every square inch of our Surapet facility to feel safe, soothing, and—most importantly—fun.

Architecture That Cares

Joint-Friendly Flooring

We chose specialized, slip-resistant surfaces because your pet’s long-term mobility matters. Whether they’re sprinting or stretching, their joints are protected from hard impacts.

Fresh Air, Always

Our hospital-grade ventilation system doesn't just "circulate" air—it replaces it. This keeps the facility smelling fresh and, more importantly, prevents the spread of airborne germs.

The Sound of Silence

Using professional acoustic dampening, we’ve created a "hush" throughout the building. This means the sound of a barking neighbor won't interrupt your pet’s afternoon nap.
